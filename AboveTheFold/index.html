    How to Play
    1. Read the summary of a real Daily Nebraskan story. The headline has been removed.
    2. Tap the headline you think is real.
    3. Three stories a day. Come back tomorrow.

    Each round uses a story that was actually published by The Daily Nebraskan. The real headline is quoted as published; the story summaries are our own, and the two fake headlines are invented for fun. Any people, mascots or squirrels named in a fake headline are in on the joke, and none of it happened. Read the full original after each answer. Above the Fold is an independent student project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Daily Nebraskan.
