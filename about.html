My story

I’m a Nebraskan through and through, raised in Omaha and now a Husker in Lincoln, working toward a double major in Advertising + Public Relations and Journalism with a minor in Psychology on the pre-law track. I love understanding what makes people tick and then finding the right words, visuals and moments to reach them.

That curiosity started in high school, where I became Editor in Chief of The Lance after starting out as a staff writer, layout designer and infographic editor. I also ran operations for our live-broadcast club, making real-time calls during streams and designing the graphic overlays for games. Since then I’ve been building the people skills that great communication depends on, from retail floors to charity fundraising, and now internships.

I swam competitively for years, including all four years on the Westside varsity team. It taught me discipline and how to win as part of a team, and I still carry that with me.
