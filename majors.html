UNL Majors Search

Look up any University of Nebraska–Lincoln major and see how many students have enrolled in it each fall since 2016.

    About the data: Fall-term student counts for each major, Fall 2016 through Fall 2025, from UNL data request DR12573. A “major” here is a major paired with a degree, so Actuarial Science (B.A.) and Actuarial Science (B.S.) are listed separately. Counts follow the “Total Students” column by first major (“Maj 1”).

    A gap in the line means the major had no students listed that fall. Programs offered in Omaha and Lincoln under the same code are combined. Major names are shown as listed by the university, in title case.
